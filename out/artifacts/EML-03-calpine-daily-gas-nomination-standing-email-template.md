## Purpose

Give the gas logistics desk a fixed template for the recurring "Calpine Daily Gas Nomination" message, so the same notice can be assembled the same way each cycle and, later, generated by a scheduled job instead of typed by hand.

## Scope

Covers the single recurring message titled "Calpine Daily Gas Nomination" as it appears in the `farmer-d/logistics` folder, its subject convention, its sender address, and its standing distribution. Does not cover meter corrections, allocation disputes, or imbalance and OBA settlement, which travel under other subjects.

## Current practice

The message is sent under a fixed, unchanging subject line, "Calpine Daily Gas Nomination" [cit_61f81d3ab9f5]. The same subject string is reused with no date, cycle, or version marker appended [cit_b843b41c8654]. It arrives from a single external address, `rickya@calpine.com`, rather than from an Enron scheduling account [cit_ba466aea576b]. It lands in the Enron gas logistics mailbox as ordinary mail, filed in the `logistics` folder [cit_4cccefa9b11b]. Successive instances are timestamped in the small hours of the morning, ahead of the working day [cit_afa39320fe4b]. Instances recur across the same series in mid-month as well as at month end [cit_8eaeeb47890d]. The series continues across calendar quarters under the identical subject [cit_4e2ac2d3f0d4]. Because every instance carries the same subject, one instance cannot be distinguished from another from the header alone [cit_70d1c123124d].

## Template

The draft below keeps the existing subject stem, which recipients already filter on [cit_61f81d3ab9f5], and adds the flow date and cycle so instances can be told apart.

```
To:      {{standing_distribution_list}}
From:    {{nominating_party_contact}}
Cc:      {{enron_scheduler}}
Subject: Calpine Daily Gas Nomination — flow date {{flow_date}}, cycle {{nomination_cycle}}

Nomination for flow date {{flow_date}}, {{nomination_cycle}} cycle.

Pipeline:            {{pipeline}}
Contract:            {{contract_number}}

Receipt point / meter:   {{receipt_meter_number}} — {{receipt_point_name}}
Delivery point / meter:  {{delivery_meter_number}} — {{delivery_point_name}}
Nominated volume:        {{nominated_volume}} {{volume_unit}}

Changes from prior cycle: {{change_summary}}

Confirm back to {{confirmation_address}} by {{confirmation_deadline}}.
<to be confirmed: whether a return confirmation was expected at all>

Supporting detail: {{attachment_or_inline_table}}
<to be confirmed: whether figures were sent inline or as an attachment>

{{sender_name}}
{{sender_phone}}
```

## Recommended change

Append the flow date and nomination cycle to the subject line, keeping "Calpine Daily Gas Nomination" as the leading stem so existing recipient rules keep matching. Publish the volumes from the deal and scheduling systems into the body as a small fixed table, so a recipient can read the figures without opening an attachment and without asking for a resend.

Move the send to a scheduled job that runs before the desk's working day, assembles the figures from their systems of record, and mails them to the standing distribution list. Keep a named human owner on the Cc line who is accountable when the job does not run, and have the job send an explicit "no nomination for this flow date" notice rather than staying silent, so a missing mail is unambiguous.

Register the distribution list in one place and reference it, rather than retyping addresses per send. Log each generated send with flow date and cycle, so a later question about what was nominated is answered from the log instead of from a mailbox search.
